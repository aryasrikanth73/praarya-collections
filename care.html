Love Your Jewellery.
Keep It Beautiful.

With the right care, your jewellery can stay beautiful for longer.

Important Note: The durability and lifespan of imitation jewellery can vary depending on the base material, plating, individual usage, and environmental exposure. Unless specifically stated for a particular product, our pieces are not permanently tarnish-proof or waterproof. Following the guidelines below will help maximize their longevity.

Essential Care Guide

Keep It Dry

Avoid prolonged contact with water, moisture, and sweat. Always remove your pieces before showering, exercising, or sleeping to prevent the plating from degrading.

Avoid Chemicals

Keep your jewellery safely away from perfumes, cosmetics, hair sprays, lotions, and household chemicals. These substances contain elements that can cause rapid tarnishing.

Store It Properly

Store each piece separately in a soft pouch or a lined jewellery box. Keep them in a cool, dry environment and avoid direct sunlight to minimize oxidation and scratching.

Wear It Last

Make your jewellery the final touch of your routine. Put it on only after applying makeup, perfume, and hair styling products so they have time to settle.

Clean Gently

After wearing, gently wipe your jewellery with a soft, dry cloth to remove body oils and dirt. Avoid harsh liquid cleaners, sonic cleaners, or abrasive materials.

Frequently Asked Questions

We highly recommend storing each piece separately in its own soft fabric pouch or a dedicated compartment in a lined jewellery box. This prevents pieces from scratching each other and minimizes exposure to the air, which can cause oxidation (tarnishing).

We strongly advise against wearing your imitation jewellery while bathing or showering. Soaps, body washes, shampoos, and plain water can leave a residue on the surface, dulling its shine. Furthermore, repeated exposure to hot water and steam can break down adhesives and degrade the metallic plating over time.

Keep it simple. After taking off your jewellery, gently wipe it down with a soft, dry microfiber or cotton cloth to remove skin oils, makeup, and dust. Do not use commercial jewellery cleaners, polishing cloths meant for solid silver/gold, toothpaste, or baking soda, as these are abrasive and will strip away the outer plating.

Imitation jewellery typically consists of a base metal coated with a thin layer of gold, silver, or rhodium plating. Over time, this plating can wear away, exposing the base metal underneath. Discoloration (tarnishing) is a natural reaction that occurs when the metals interact with air, moisture, the natural pH levels of your skin, and chemicals found in daily cosmetic products. Proper care significantly delays this process.
